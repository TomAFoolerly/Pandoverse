#### Essence

We'll start at the bottom with Essence.  Essence is the fundamental particle of the universe upon which everything else is made. Smaller than Quarks or anything else you've heard of, and far more powerful.

Essence in its natural state is a chaotic rapidly changing particle. always changing its physics and appearance. not a good basis for life huh?

Well through chance a few of these particles managed to tame itself long enough for life to form, and Throughout a long series of cosmic evolution of these particle specilizing and forming patterns life emerged. Forming generations of reality creatures and smaller beings like ourselves.

**Appearance and layout**
Essence has no Anatomy or appearance that isn't programmed. it's always something else and it is everything that we see. 

*Essence types*

Essence has 2 conditions
1. Unstable: This is Essences natural state. The particle randomly flips and changes around its internal configuration, and can spontaneously switch between hot and cold states.

2. Stable: Essence tamed. It stays and follows roughly what its patterns its been programmed to do. 

Essence is able to generate the world we know through its vibrations. Generating the basic physics that make up everything. This vibrating has three properties. 

We'll go over those below. starting with its first one. Its Charge.
**Charge**
Essence needs something to power itself however. Some kind of force to make it move and carry out functions. The universal currency used here are its Charge. All essence particles carry these this Charge to different levels, and This is represented by a spectrum of cold-hot depending on how much charge it has. Charge is the amplitude of the vibrations an essence has determining how aggressive it vibrates.
Another interesting property is that the hotter essence becomes the less mass it has as essence turns to mostly to energy at its hottest states.

1. Cold: Low charge state. Cold essence is fairly non-changing, and will occupy and space and carry out a function. This is most of what pando's more solid systems and life use as their base building blocks. Cold essence Will capture hot essence which will orbit around it as the cold essence gradually leeching charge away for its necessary functions. 

2. Hot: High charge state. Commonly called Sparks. Sparks will naturally zip around finding cold essence to orbit. However most cold essence can only hold so many sparks, and upon reaching its maximum will pass to the next cold essence it can find. Hot essence is used as pando's go to communication agent for messages between essence. 

 

Unless specified we'll always be talking about the stable variety of Essence.

Pando has a circulatory system to pass hot essence to where its needed, and then move it along when it starts losing too much charge. Always ensuring all things possess a fresh charge and will contiune flowing. if it didn't all essence would lose their charge and become just a solid mass. Cold essence that runs out of sparks will freeze into its last position and remain there until further sparks arrive.





#### QNA
Essence once stablized (and we'll get to how thats done) can begin to hold instructions. How these instructions are recieved is through stimuli. when a stable essence recieves any kind of contact or interaction with another particle it stores that interaction as a sequence. over eons essence begin to store longer and more complicated sequences until patterns emerged. Eventually essence began to store reactions such as how it should behave, and if it should accept or ignore further instructions or even remove recieved stimuli. allowing it to create stable patterns. 

This created what we call QNA. or Quintessential Nucleic Adaptation. The "DNA" of reality. These patterns have created a wide variety of life and physics, and life has taken full advantage. 

Qna has led to specilization in essences functions. More specific patterns focused on narrow jobs allows for Less room for errors that can result from enormously long generalized instructions. this creates differentiation in essence. meaning most of pando is only programmed to work in certain ways. leading to the separate corpora we see following a similar idea. 

Essence can store an almost unlimited amount of stimuli, the sophisitication drastically increases exponentially as you do so as contradictions between instructions can increase. Leading to it being more efficient to specialize

**essence signaling**
Now to use what we learned. Lets describe how essence talks to each other.

QNA provides essence with instructions on how it should act, and Charge provides the energy to act on these instructions. So when one part of Pando wants to talk to another it sends out Sparks(hot essence) these sparks travel through pando's pando using QNA to tell them how and where to go.


They find what they're looking for by looking for someone's QNA address. This is a unique part of the their QNA acting like their personal ID code. Pando's QNA holds many checks to verify that its working with a friendly being within itself. this isn't to say it can't be fooled however. As well considering the enormous size of pando it does allow for some variance in the address considering that its destination might not be in the same place or conditions when it sent out.

But through addresses and qna to send energy and matter throughout its body allowing for a creature of its size to exist.
