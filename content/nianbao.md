_Vapour_  
The Shantei being enormous creatures have an equally enormous need to process these volumes of information and so a specialized essence was developed just for handling that. This is called Vapour.

Vapour is a differentiated essence for use exclusively in the Nianbao. It forms an aerogel like substance which fills the entirety of the Corpus. Its the gel that allows for the conductivity of the Nianbao to function.

Vapour is in its natural state neutral. it has almost no properties other than sticking together and having a mild resistance when pressure is applied. My colleague has also informed me it has a citrus like flavour. This i have confirmed.

Vapour however isn't here to fill space. Vapour is fantastic at intaking and outputting data. when exposed to stimuli that passes a certain threshold (discussed later) Vapour will take on new properties and change its shape, color and even how it behaves. Upon testing this threshold seems to be an immediate barrier as it will "forget" stimuli mere seconds after the previous one. Likely to prevent constant noise and to reset the threshold for new stimuli once the old has passed.

The three discovered properties we've discovered of Vapour are as follows. Keep in mind with Vapour being everywhere these can be considered universal qualities of the Corpus

**Density**:  
Density is how much information is being processed. Info in the Nianbao has a weight to it. The more data is being processed and more data in one area the Vapour there becomes physically heavy. This in large enough quantities creates a gravity like effect in the Nianbao helping to form globes. which we'll get to later.

This is interesting as it implies the weight of information creating a weight effect on nearby Vapour despite not directly holding that information.

Despite what you might expect density seems to disproportionately affect larger objects more than smaller ones. Trapping Globes while allowing for individuals to escape the gravity of large objects. fascinating

**valence**:  
Valence is how the information was felt by those involved. If the information is about a human celebrating a birthday for example the information would carry with it the valence of happiness. Valence carries with it properties similar to that of the periodic elements. Like how iron can form solid bars and oxygen a breathable gas valence in the Nianbao can create a wide variety of effects.

Valence seems to exist as Pando is both interested in how the creatures within it feels and is itself a living creature with potential emotions and feelings. It seems emotions themselves carry physical and chemical power within the Nianbao

**Intensity**:  
Intensity represents how intense that information is felt. Intensity provides the raw energy of the data. If the valence provides a strong effect intensity amplifies it. as well intensity is used by several creatures of the Nianbao for power and energy. If no one felt anything during the information's recording there is a basic intensity Pando applies to all data.

Intensity seems to be charge provided by the pulse at a default level for all Vapour. with the default amount varying based on the thought stars size and luminosity (more on those beautiful wonders later)

_reactivity threshold_  
As we know there's certain thresholds for how Vapour reacts, and its thanks to these that the Nianbao is not a chaotic maelstrom of constantly changing enviroments. Most of the time Its a fairly stable place.

Vapour builds up a tolerance to a certain level of frequent stimuli. This creates an average that can increase or decrease as needed. This threshold cuts out small stimuli where the Vapour will not react and allow the stimuli to occur unremarked upon. This allows for the everyday actions of the Nianbao residents to continue without worrying about their smallest action creating chaos.

angels and other elites however can modify the command center of the Nianbao somite filter selectively what stimuli they do and don't want rather than relying on frequency.

Now that we know this we can begin to describe how the Nianbao actually works.

#### Glamous

We know that Somites have command centers, and within the Nianbao they work a little differently. These Nianbao command centers are called Glamous.

The Glamous is veinlike tower that forms the bottom of the hub. It grows from this floor upwards and from it the thought star forms at the top of the tower. these veins are typically a dark green in appearance.

The Glamous operates as a processor for the input and output of the hub and the thought star holds what it currently being worked on. All data that needs processing is circulated from the thought star into the Glamous where complicated organic machinery decides what is important and what is not and then passes the relevant info back to the thought star for Flare transfer.

Glamous are connected via dedicated corridors to all nearby Somites. The veins of the heart will have these corridors wrapped around itself and it will be deposited into the Glamous via a corridor that fires directly into a gill positioned below the Glamous.

In another way different from hun-po Somites which are mostly autonomous Nianbao Somites are almost always run by angels to quickly and effectively make decisions on info if something serious arrives. **fae culture**

I've discussed heavily with the local fae and they're organized as such.

1. Pod. a local grouping typically of 5-10ish individual fae. Typically related, but not always.
    
    1. Fae will sometimes take on orphaned fae or loners that make sufficient contributions
2. clan: a grouping of pods. can be big or small grouping. typically have a council or clan leader depending on ruling style. always a matriarch.
    
3. Community: a grouping of dozens or even hundreds of clans. will usually have a guiding Queen that is elected by the clans. However some clans have called for a new style with a council of elected representatives
    

The fae regardless of social group size are led by a matriarch. Each one embodying the form of their local culture in their nianbao form. Adopting styles and architecture based on a mix of their former hun-po culture and the styles they've picked up along the way.

In the nianbao these mothers guide young fae in their forms and on occasion shift to their hun-po bubble to guide young Oneiri on how best to seduce mortals and help the young in their first molts. as they must molt once to shed their current forms to form a node and fully survive in the nianbao.

These Fae prefer to migrate from globe to globe based on Clay feasting, and to bring down manifesting cost. most fae also have migration patterns that follow within the hun-po too and they will follow both regularly.

Fae in the past would migrate based on seasons. each fae group now migrates searching for their preferred data from globes. searching for their specific preferred diet of clay. Each Clan generally has their own preferred diet of primary clay mixtures.

Their sons are warriors responsible for clearing out oneiri and pollen when needed. Or any other threats, but they always stay loyal to their mothers in which they have strong familial relationships with.

the fae are masters of shifting between the hun-po and nianbao, and can do so cheaply because of their still existing ties to the mortal world.
